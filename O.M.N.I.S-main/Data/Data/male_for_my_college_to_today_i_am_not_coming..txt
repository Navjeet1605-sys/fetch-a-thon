**Sample Absence Note for Your College**

---

**[Your Name]**  
[Your Student ID]  
[Course/Program]  
[Date]  

**To:**  
[Professor’s Name / Dean / Registrar]  
[College/University Name]  
[Department or Office]  

Dear **[Professor’s/Dean’s Title] [Last Name]**,

I am writing to inform you that I will be unable to attend classes on **[Date]**. Unfortunately, I am experiencing [brief reason—e.g., a sudden illness, a personal emergency, a family matter] that requires my immediate attention and prevents me from being present on campus.

I understand the importance of maintaining attendance and staying on track with coursework. I will make every effort to obtain lecture notes, recordings, or any assignments distributed during my absence, and I will submit any required work promptly upon my return.

If a medical certificate or any additional documentation is needed, please let me know, and I will provide it at the earliest convenience.

Thank you for your understanding.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature (if sending a hard copy)]  
**[Your Printed Name]**  
[Email Address] | [Phone Number]  

---  

**How to Use This Template**

1. **Fill in the placeholders** (your name, student ID, date, professor’s name, etc.).  
2. **Add a brief, honest reason** for your absence—no need to go into excessive detail.  
3. **Send or hand‑deliver** the note to the appropriate office or professor.  
4. **Follow up** by gathering any missed material and submitting any required assignments as soon as possible.  

Feel free to modify the tone to match the formality of your institution. Good luck!
